Bermuda Triangle

700,000 SQUARE KILOMETERS OF UNEXPLAINED DEVIATION

"The sea is dangerous and its storms are terrible, but these obstacles have never been sufficient reason to remain ashore." — Ferdinand Magellan

Scientific Theory

Methane Hydrates

Enormous pockets of natural gas trapped beneath the seafloor can erupt. These bubbles reduce water density, causing ships to sink like stones, and can even stall aircraft engines if they rise into the sky.

Conspiracy

The Lost City of Atlantis

Some believe the ruins of Atlantis lie beneath the Bimini Road. Proponents suggest that "energy crystals" used by the Atlanteans are still active, interfering with ship electronics and navigation.

Scientific Theory

Magnetic Anomalies

The Triangle is one of the places on Earth where "True North" and "Magnetic North" align. This "agonic line" can cause compasses to give confusing readings to inexperienced navigators.

Conspiracy

Extraterrestrial Portals

UFO enthusiasts suggest the area is a "Vile Vortex"—an interdimensional doorway where aliens harvest humans and vessels for study. This gained popularity after the disappearance of Flight 19.

Scientific Theory

Rogue Waves

Satellite data confirms that waves up to 100 feet tall (30 meters) can occur here. These "walls of water" are sudden, unpredictable, and powerful enough to snap a cargo ship in half.

Statistical Reality

The "No Mystery" Theory

The World Wildlife Fund found that the Triangle isn't even in the top 10 most dangerous shipping lanes. Given the massive traffic, the number of accidents is statistically average.
