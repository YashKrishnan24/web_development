The Constitution of India

A simple, interactive guide to the supreme law of the land.

📜

History

Originated from the desire for self-rule. The Cabinet Mission Plan (1946) set the stage to transfer power from the British and build a framework for a sovereign, independent nation.

🏛️

Formation

Drafted by the Constituent Assembly. It took exactly 2 years, 11 months, and 18 days to complete. It was adopted on Nov 26, 1949, and officially enforced on Jan 26, 1950.

🖋️

Main Figures

  • Dr. B.R. Ambedkar: Chairman of the Drafting Committee.
  • Dr. Rajendra Prasad: President of the Assembly.
  • Jawaharlal Nehru: Introduced the Objective Resolution.
  • B.N. Rau: Constitutional Advisor.
🌍

Impact

It is the world's longest written constitution. It established India as a Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic Republic, guaranteeing Fundamental Rights to all citizens.
