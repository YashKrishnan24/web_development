How Do Windmills Work?

It's like a giant fan, but in reverse! Instead of using electricity to make wind, it uses wind to make electricity.

Show Me How! ↓

The 3 Simple Steps

🌬️

1. Catch the Breeze

When the wind blows, it pushes the curved blades, making them spin around the center hub.

⚙️

2. Turn the Gears

Inside the box (nacelle), the spinning blades turn a shaft connected to a generator full of magnets.

💡

3. Make Magic

Those spinning magnets create an electric current that travels down cables to power your home!

Fun Fact! 🌍

  • ✅ One windmill can power up to 1,500 homes.
  • ✅ They are taller than the Statue of Liberty!
  • ✅ Wind energy never runs out.
