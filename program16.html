Origins

The Birth
of a Giant

Cricket was introduced to India in the 18th century by British sailors in Cambay (1721). The Parsi community formed India's first cricket club in Bombay in 1848, sparking a rivalry with European clubs.

The Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) was founded on December 4, 1928, in Delhi. India played their maiden Test match on June 25, 1932, against England at Lord's — the ground they would one day make tremble with their genius.

Lala Amarnath became the first Indian to score a Test century. The team wore white; the nation dreamed in colour. From borrowed bats to billion-dollar contracts — the arc of Indian cricket is the most dramatic in sport.

Cricket's Debt to India

How India
Changed
Everything

Format Revolution

The IPL Effect

Launched in 2008 by the BCCI, the Indian Premier League fundamentally altered the economics of cricket. With a franchise model, player auctions, and celebrity ownership, it transformed cricketers into global entertainers earning $10M+ annually. Every major cricket board followed India's template.

Financial Dominance

The Money Game

India controls approximately 70-80% of global cricket's revenues. The BCCI's media rights deal for 2023-27 crossed ₹48,390 crore — making India's domestic cricket more valuable than many international sporting leagues. India doesn't just play; India finances the game.

Batting Innovation

Reinventing Attack

From Sachin's straight drive to Rohit's pull shot to Virat's cover drive — Indian batsmen defined what technical excellence in run-scoring looks like. The "Vishwanath flick", the "Tendulkar punch" — techniques studied in academies worldwide.

Global Influence

Spin Mastery

The "spin quartet" of Bedi, Prasanna, Venkataraghavan, and Chandrasekhar in the 1970s elevated wrist-spin to an art form. Anil Kumble's 10 wickets in an innings vs Pakistan (1999) stands as cricket's most ruthless bowling performance. India made spin sexy.

Cultural Phenomenon

Cricket is
India's Religion

When India plays, 800 million people watch. Television channels broadcast matches on screens in village squares. Offices go quiet. Streets empty. The entire subcontinent holds its breath as one.

India's cricketers are cultural deities — their birthdays celebrated, their centuries converting into national holidays in home states. Sachin Tendulkar's retirement triggered a national mourning that rivalled no sporting event in history.

Cricket shapes India's identity, politics, and economy. The 2003 and 2011 World Cup finals generated the highest simultaneous TV viewership in human history. A single Virat Kohli Instagram post earns more engagement than most world leader announcements.

The IPL is now the second-highest attended sporting league globally, behind only the NFL. 13 cities, 10 franchises, and a season that rivals the NFL Super Bowl in global advertising rates.
