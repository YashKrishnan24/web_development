Global Events

What Happened This February?

From festivals and elections to scientific breakthroughs and international summits, February was packed with moments that matter.

🏮
Feb 1 – 15 · Asia

Lunar New Year — Year of the Snake

Billions across China, Vietnam, South Korea, and Southeast Asia welcomed the Year of the Snake. Festivities included parades, dragon dances, family reunions, and fireworks. The Snake is associated with wisdom, intuition, and transformation.

🌏 Asia-Pacific
⚽
Feb 2 · USA

Super Bowl LIX — Philadelphia Eagles

The Philadelphia Eagles defeated the Kansas City Chiefs in Super Bowl LIX, ending Kansas City's bid for a historic three-peat. The halftime show and record-breaking TV viewership made it one of the most-watched events in American history.

🌎 North America
🎭
Feb 4 – Mar 4 · Brazil

Rio Carnival 2025

Rio de Janeiro exploded with colour as the world's largest carnival drew over 2 million visitors. Samba schools competed in spectacular parades at the Sambadrome, while street parties (blocos) filled every neighbourhood day and night.

🌎 South America
🎬
Feb 13–23 · Germany

Berlin International Film Festival

The Berlinale, one of the world's top film festivals, screened over 400 films from 70 countries. The Golden Bear award crowned the best film as filmmakers, actors, and cinephiles gathered in Berlin from across the globe.

🌍 Europe
🌿
Feb · Global

UN Climate Talks Continue

Nations continued negotiations under the Paris Agreement framework, with February bringing key preparatory sessions for COP30. Discussions focused on carbon markets, climate finance for developing nations, and reducing methane emissions.

🌐 Global
🚀
Feb · USA

SpaceX Starship Test Milestones

SpaceX continued its ambitious Starship testing programme, pushing reusability boundaries and taking steps toward crewed lunar missions under NASA's Artemis programme. Each test flight captured global attention as humanity inches closer to deep space travel.

🌎 North America
🗳️
Feb 23 · Germany

German Federal Elections 2025

Germany held a snap federal election following the collapse of the coalition government. The CDU/CSU led by Friedrich Merz emerged as the largest party, marking a significant political shift and raising questions about Europe's largest economy.

🌍 Europe
🏔️
Feb · India

Maha Kumbh Mela 2025 Continues

The world's largest human gathering continued through February in Prayagraj, India. Over 400 million pilgrims attended across its span — a number exceeding the population of most countries — setting a new world record for attendance.

🌏 South Asia
🤖
Feb · Global

AI Regulation Debates Heat Up

The European Union's AI Act came into effect with new provisions, while the US and UK engaged in transatlantic talks on AI governance. Governments worldwide grappled with how to regulate AI models, deepfakes, and autonomous systems.

🌐 Global

In Focus

Spotlight Events

Two events that defined headlines around the world this February.

🏆
Sports · Feb 2025

Africa Cup of Nations — Final Showdown

The African football world came together as top nations competed for the continent's most prestigious trophy. Passionate supporters, upsets, and memorable goals made the tournament a showcase of African footballing talent on the global stage.

Diplomacy · Feb 2025

Munich Security Conference

World leaders, defence ministers, and foreign policy experts gathered in Munich for the annual security conference. The agenda was dominated by the ongoing conflict in Ukraine, NATO's future role, and rising tensions in the Indo-Pacific region.
